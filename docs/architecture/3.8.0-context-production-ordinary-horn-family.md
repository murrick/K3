## Separate consistency witnesses

Preflight requires native-bound ordinary declarations, Horn clauses (at most
one positive literal), and excludes universal units. The terminal audit covers
all original, carried and observed local clauses and actual ground unit donors.
For each cut choose P, the actual proved positive ground units. Interpret all
other ground atoms as false in this witness, across all admissible values,
including unmentioned ones. This choice does not assert negative knowledge.

For any Horn instance with all body atoms in P, the complete local donor audit
requires its residual head to be observed. An enabled unbound universal unit
cannot certify. A grounded head therefore belongs to P. A headless constraint
with all body atoms in P requires a collision and cannot certify. Opposing
negative units are checked explicitly. Thus the chosen interpretation satisfies
every clause in this particular cut's augmented ledger. Dormant universal rules
need no enumeration of the domain. Each cut has its own witness: raw sibling
opposition is allowed and sibling facts are not communal X facts.

This proves consistency of the audited ledgers and closure under unit-donor
reductions. It does NOT prove closure under transport of all X-dependent
clause-to-clause consequences.

## Concrete missing composition boundary

Use ground declarations (the quantified version reproduces the same boundary):

```
X: !~a(1) || ~b(1) || ~c(1);
A: !d(1) -> a(1);
B: !d(1) -> b(1);
C: !d(1); !c(1);
```

The current reduction family stops after two rounds, carrying ~a(1)||~b(1).
Every current cut has a model, so the scoped reduction certificate is valid.
However X and A entail H = ~d(1)||~b(1)||~c(1) by resolution on a.
Transport H to C: its actual d,c entail ~b. Transport ~b to B: its rule entails
~d. Transport ~d to C: it contradicts actual d. Every transported consequence
is X-dependent; this argument does not import raw sibling facts into X.
The driver independently verifies each entailment on all 16 truth assignments
and verifies the reduction pass does not invent the absent d-containing clause.
This counterexample prohibits using this certificate for general admission.

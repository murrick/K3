# Stage3e: coverage of observed productive positive Horn proof pools

## Concrete missing-proof counterexample

A gives q(w) from X's p(w). A B pass q&&flag->out gives out(w) with
{X0,A1,B2}. Later another A declaration adds a second q proof, {X0,A4}, without
replaying B. The resulting detached pool is entirely sound and contains both
q and out, but out still has only its first proof. It is not closed under B:
{X0,A4,B2} at depth2 is missing. The new checker refuses that pool; only an actual
next native B pass supplies the second out proof and earns coverage.

A separate control builds q at depth2 from two clauses of one source declaration.
The internal obligation checker rejects this proof when asked to discharge a
depth1 obligation with the same exact source group. Atom presence and source-set
presence alone do not establish minimum-depth closure.

## Soundness plus closure, not a second inference engine

exportCoveredNext first invokes exportNext on the owning ContextWitnessSeedReplay.
Thus every candidate Item/proof is imported sound evidence or reconstructed from
actual native applications and materializations. No caller-supplied alleged proof
pool can be tagged covered. Failure returns no candidate prefix.

The entire supplied declaration cut is independently compiled/validated through
ContextWitnessDeclarationCut. The owning pass now retains its exact original
input Packet and exposes an immutable snapshot of captured producer Inputs only
after successful native settlement. sameDeclarations compares the supplied and
owning source sets plus all alpha-equivalent clause shapes (ignoring list order
and native allocation IDs). Reusing a Source label with a different rule, omitting
a declaration, or supplying a different target cut refuses coverage before closure.
This binds the certificate to actual declarations, not just their labels. It still
does not authenticate completeness against a live Context revision.

The checker accepts positive carried/result literals and fact-free definite Horn
clauses only. Every head variable must appear in its body. Shared source-cut,
ordinary argument, two-variable/three-literal and declaration bounds remain in
force; existential/function/system/calculated cases do not gain support.

For every clause, all body instances in the complete candidate pool are matched
with one consistent canonical Origin/fixed-value assignment. For each match,
every Cartesian combination of whole donor proof alternatives forms an obligation:

- The exact positive head instance is already present.
- It has a proof with exactly the union of donor sources and the producer Source.
- That proof's depth is at most max(donor depths)+1.

A strict superset of sources cannot discharge an exact-source obligation. A
shallower sound proof can discharge a longer redundant derivation with the same
sources; cycles therefore need not enumerate arbitrarily long proof paths.
If an uncovered group would exceed the depth bound, the result is INCOMPLETE,
not truncated coverage. Every candidate proof itself must respect the depth bound.
The checker records only a successful obligation count; it never creates an Atom,
Item, Support, missing source alternative or shorter proof. All work is bounded.

## Why postfixed proof-pool checking is sufficient in this scope

For positive, range-restricted, function-free definite Horn rules, every new
positive literal proof can be normalized to forward Horn applications. If there
were a first missing result, all its donor literals would already belong to the
candidate, and the exhaustive obligations would expose its absence. The same
induction applies to exact source sets and improved depths, using the sound
carried seed ledger as the starting evidence. This certifies closure relative to
that ledger and these declarations, not globally minimal historical proofs from
other omitted source snapshots. The all-true model excludes forced negative
literals in this positive scope.

Range restriction ensures any new variable value comes from a matched body;
ordinary head constants are checked semantically. The finite operation enumerates
body matches of a finite predicate extension, not an artificially finite universe.
@x still covers every admissible and unmentioned value. Head-only variables are
refused even for disabled bodies here; the older dormant certificate retains its
separate weaker requirements.

## Certificate and barrier behavior

A successful Packet retains observedStep=true and adds an exact coveredCut tag,
with the owning input identity, captured producer labels, and unchanged proved
Items/support depths. Family checks the tagged cut alongside its existing basis,
parent-X and actual-producer checks. The family union drops branch coverage tags:
sibling results can enable further work, so coverage must be renewed at the next
barrier. Old covered results cannot be reused against another input identity.
Legacy exportNext remains observed-proof-only and is still mergeable; this stage
does not silently convert every family merge into a coverage verdict.

No-observed-application passes remain INCOMPLETE through this productive path.
Existing structural empty certificates are their separately proved completion
paths. MERGED, changed=false and one covered branch are not global SATURATED or
ACCEPTED decisions. Full family fixed-point integration is next, with exact
snapshots and collision qualification still required before runtime admission.

## Controls and limits

The46 new controls cover actual chains, carried alternatives, two-premise
Cartesian products, the missing-alternative and deeper-proof counterexamples,
exact owning source/shape binding, alpha equivalence and declaration order,
missing declarations, depth/work/event stops, no native application, head-only
variables, signed/unit/negative-head refusals, fixed head values, self-X cuts,
covered/empty family aggregation, tag dropping, stale barriers and closed owners.

General signed interacting cuts, non-Horn residuals, new witnesses, witness-free
native application gaps, full X rule/scalar snapshots and live revision
authentication remain unsupported. Main/Core and runtime authoring gate are
unchanged. Question3 query/hypothesis/orphans stays open.

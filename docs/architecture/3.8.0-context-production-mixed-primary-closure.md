## Whole Source mechanism

Positive ground unit clauses are primary evidence only when the proof pool
already contains that literal with exactly the same original singleton Source
at depth0. Horn schema validation checks this prerequisite; a same-valued fact
from another Source is insufficient, and an inferred depth1 fact cannot replace
primary authentication. Universal units with head variables are not ground
primary facts and remain unsupported by this closure path.

Whole native-bound primary rows may now contribute positive ground unit clauses
to the seed ledger while their complete original declarations remain producers.
A new `PRIVATE_SEEDS_AND_PRODUCER` disposition records both roles and one whole
Source. There is no generated clause Source or rewritten implication. X already
has its corresponding `X_SEEDS_AND_PRODUCER` disposition. Every original clause
is captured and validated; ground facts require their authenticated depth0 proof,
and each implication retains the existing positive/range-restricted Horn checks.
Round obligations and final closure include both fact and implication clauses.

A fact does not waive another unsupported clause of the same Source: a head-only
variable still leaves full coverage INCOMPLETE. Signed ordinary constraints stay
in the original declaration/scan path; signed bundles, mixed conditional witness
forms, functions and multiple origins in a literal are not implicitly widened.
A ground conjunct is entailed by its original whole native-bound assertion, so
its support always names that assertion; the remaining clauses are never dropped
from admission accounting.

Private ground facts remain in the owning local pool. Only verified X-dependent
products export to common X. Ground facts belonging to X retain their existing
common primary proofs. Their depth is0; ordinary derived conclusions continue
to add inference depth. All prior work/events/depth/rank/atom/support/whole-row
bounds and the immutable common/local round barriers remain.

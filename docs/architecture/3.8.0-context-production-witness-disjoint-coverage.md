# Stage3c: certified empty predicate-disjoint Horn branches

## Why disjointness alone is insufficient

From a packet using p, consider declarations `a(x,1)->b(x,1)` and
`a(x,1)->~b(x,1)`. Their predicates are disjoint from p, yet they entail
`~a(x,1)` at every value. The native control accepts the rules together with an
unrelated existential p, then rejects the previously unmentioned `a(17,1)`.
A declaration module can have consequences without an initial fact in its own
vocabulary. Absence of observed witness applications cannot certify emptiness.

## Exact certificate and proof

ContextWitnessDisjointCoverage proves an empty branch for this restricted
literal-proof surface, relative to the entire supplied declaration snapshot:

- The sealed input is a pure detached literal pool with no exact opposite pair.
  Canonical origins and fixed semantic values are retained.
- Every supplied declaration has passed whole-rule/source-cut validation.
- No predicate name anywhere in the declarations occurs anywhere in the input
  packet. The test is deliberately conservative across arity and fixed arguments.
- Every clause is fact-free definite Horn: exactly one positive literal and at
  least one negative literal. Thus it represents a positive-premise implication
  with a positive head; universal/ground units and negative heads are refused.

The disjoint declaration module has both the uniform all-false and uniform
all-true interpretation of its predicates as models, over the entire domain.
Both extend any model of the old relational literal packet. Therefore the old
vocabulary gains no new semantic literal consequence, and neither sign of any
new-vocabulary atom is forced, including at fresh/unmentioned values or at
carried witnesses. This does not enumerate a finite domain or restrict @x.

For the connected clause-resolution/application proof surface, source/proof
alternatives of old literals also remain unchanged. No declaration literal can
resolve against a packet literal, and the consistent isolated Horn module cannot
produce an empty clause to bridge that separation. Arbitrary irrelevant-premise
weakening is not part of the qualified proof language. No residual clauses or
universal rules are claimed as transported literal results.

This is a structural conservative-extension proof, not a zero-event heuristic,
forward-chaining engine, or SAT test. Native all-false/all-true controls illustrate
the models; the certificate depends on checking every clause, not those samples.

## Sealed declaration and family integration

Whole-rule capture was extracted unchanged from Stage3b into
ContextWitnessDeclarationCut and is shared by collision and coverage checks.
A fresh disposable compiler/capture verifies source bindings and the complete
original Rules before their shapes become evidence. Existing bounds remain:
1..64 declarations, at most8 conjunctive clauses per Rule,2 universal variables,
3 literals per clause, ordinary fixed TERM/universal TVARIABLE arguments only.
Parent X and the target cut must match exact context/revision/view/role; at least
one actual declaration from the target is required. Empty alleged snapshots,
wrong cuts, duplicate sources, functions/existentials, capture gaps and later
unsupported declarations all refuse the certificate without a prefix.

exportDisjointEmpty invokes the verifier itself and alone can create this new
private completed-branch kind. The output copies the exact input Items and proof
groups, records the exact input identity, target cut and captured producer Source
set, and has a fresh output identity. It is explicitly not an observed native
STEP. The family barrier accepts either a verified native STEP or this certified
empty branch, retaining its exact basis/cut/producer checks. An absent native
application still returns INCOMPLETE through exportNext; no automatic fallback
or unproved empty-result flag is exposed to callers.

A mixed productive-A/empty-B family now completes without inventing a B product.
An all-certified-empty family yields MERGED with changed=false. Aggregate pools
still cannot masquerade as completed branches; an empty certificate cannot be
reused at another input barrier. Work/event/depth stops publish no packet; carried
derived depths are checked and never reset.

and authentication against a live Context revision are still trusted-caller work;
full X rules/scalar snapshots are not silently recovered from a witness Packet.

has no applications. General no-product completion, rule-rule residual coverage,

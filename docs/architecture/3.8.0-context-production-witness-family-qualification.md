# Stage3g: full supplied-cut collision checks around positive family discovery

## Concrete barrier refutations

X carries p(w,1),flag(w,1). A:p->q and B:p->r produce q and r at a common
barrier. C:q&&r->~flag then has a falsified clause instance with exact proof
{X0,A1,B2,C3}, depth2. The new family qualification protocol reports COLLISION at
barrier1, also under reversed cut order. If B instead needs q&&flag->out and C
has out->~flag, the refutation appears at barrier2 with depth3. Collision checks
must repeat after changed barriers, not only inspect the initial input or final
positive fixed point.

These are X-dependent witness products checked against C's own source cut.
C's raw declarations are never replayed as sibling knowledge in A or B. A
control with opposite raw universal units in two foreign cuts and no q in X is
INCOMPLETE, not a fabricated X collision. A-B contradictions remain permitted.

## Three outcomes with explicit incomplete coverage

ContextWitnessFamilyQualification.run takes the immutable Stage3f Plan, sound
initial Packet and bounded Limits. The Plan still seals every supplied original
branch/source declaration and identical X declarations across branches. Before
any proof is published, the entire supplied family passes the shared generic
universal Rule/source-cut capture boundary. Unsupported later existential or
other unrepresentable declarations suppress any earlier collision prefix.
The discovery frontier is currently restricted to positive carried literals.

Original whole source declarations are classified for the existing positive
Horn discovery subset. All clauses of a declaration must qualify; a mixed
conjunctive declaration is not silently split under the same Source label.
Every excluded Source is retained explicitly in an immutable uncovered set.
A constraint-only cut can be absent from the producer subset but is always
present in the complete-cut collision checks. Shared X declarations retained
by the producer subset must remain identical in all its branches.

The protocol has exactly these qualification outcomes:

- COLLISION: a checked falsified-clause certificate from one or more original
  cuts at one exact common Packet identity. It binds the full supplied Plan and
  original input identity, and includes the barrier number and immutable complete
  source/depth evidence. No accepted/result knowledge pool is returned.
- FIXED_POINT: every supplied declaration belongs to the covered positive Horn
  family, its scoped common fixed point is proved, and all direct collision scans
  completed, including the final unchanged barrier. This remains relative to
  supplied snapshots, not runtime admission or general consistency coverage.
- INCOMPLETE: no refutation was established within the supported search/bounds,
  or any supplied declaration lacks productive coverage. Even a successfully
  closed positive discovery subset cannot produce FIXED_POINT when uncovered is
  nonempty. No positive search pool, partial fixed-point trace or collision prefix
  is published as a result.

Omitting an unsupported rule from discovery is only a sound way to search for
positive refutations from a subset of the knowledge. It is never a compatible,
SATURATED or ACCEPTED verdict. Rules can still have unsearched signed/residual
consequences even when no direct instance is falsified.

## Complete scans at initial and merged barriers

The existing positive fixed-point driver now has an optional barrier check hook,
called after each full family merge and before its fixed-point decision. Its
ordinary run path remains unchanged. The qualification wrapper also checks the
initial packet at barrier0, allowing an already proved conflict to be reported
without any native pass, even if no positive self-X discovery subset exists.

Each monitor calls the Stage3b refutation checker separately with an original
complete supplied cut. Its new scanned flag distinguishes an exhaustive bounded
direct-instance scan from unsupported/resource-stopped verification. scanned=true
without evidence remains Status.INCOMPLETE in the refutation checker: it is not
an inference-coverage or consistency claim. A monitor commits a collision list
only after every supplied cut's scan at this barrier completed. A failed later
scan discards the provisional evidence prefix.

The positive discovery loop uses only actually observed, fully covered native
passes or separately proved unchanged Horn pools. Constraint-only cuts cannot
manufacture empty branch completion: they remain uncovered, so their omission
can only support discovery of a positive collision. Work/event/depth/round stops
publish no result prefix. There is no physical parallelism or live Context write.

## Scope and polarity

`!($x p(x))->q();` is equivalent to `!@x p(x)->q();` when x does not occur
free in q. Negating the existential antecedent yields a universal negative p
literal; this use of existential surface syntax needs no witness object.
The equivalence holds over all admissible values, not merely the active domain.
It contrasts with the previous universal antecedent, whose negative occurrence
requires unsupported conditional-witness structure.

A76-check native scope driver compares nested existential, prenex universal
and alpha-renamed existential forms, both atom polarities and zero-arity or
ground-unary q. Every form binds as exactly one whole primary Source/one clause,
one universal variable, two literals, exact input/head signs and ground head
arity. Entry kind is CLAUSES with zero witnesses. The original declaration
remains whole; syntax normalization does not grant a new existential witness
capability or permit free-variable capture in the consequent.

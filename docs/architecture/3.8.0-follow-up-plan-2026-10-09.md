## 1. Code hygiene

Begin with an inventory of dead code, temporary workarounds, obsolete comments,
repeated initialization and misplaced diagnostics. Remove only demonstrably
unused code. Review responsibilities in Mind, Linker, Console and federation
support before extracting cohesive components. Consolidate scattered settings
and initialization where their ownership is clear.

Use small, independently reviewable changes and appropriate regression checks.
Preserve inference behavior, correlated bindings, secondary variable children,
resultative formal hypotheses and concrete negative-proof Values. Do not turn
structural cleanup into a change of logical semantics.

## 2. Long-session stability

The next functional qualification remains repeated connections, queries,
rollback, publication and historical fork in a sustained session. Code hygiene
must not displace or silently close this work.

Check that working productions and temporary objects do not accumulate across
repeated equivalent cycles, that rollback releases child state, and that pinned
revisions retain their truth and Values as CURRENT advances. Distinguish valid
retained revision state from unsupported computed-state retention; do not use
heap size alone as proof of a leak or successful reclamation.

This extends the John/Tom regression: the individual stale-production case is
fixed; repeated lifecycle transitions still need qualification. Record cycle
counts, semantic assertions, retained-state measurements and any limitations.
Broader pin/commit/fork/cycle stress and federation performance measurements
remain part of the follow-up qualification.

## 3. Remaining federation semantics and M5

After long-session stability, continue global qualification/acceptance of foreign
assumptions and sufficient hypotheses across the complete federation, followed
by M5 identity and ownership work. Their implementation is not implied by the
integrated checkpoint. Consult the architecture contracts before choosing the
next implementation slice.

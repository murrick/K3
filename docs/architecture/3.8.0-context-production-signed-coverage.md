# Stage3u: observed signed unit-inference coverage

ContextGroundRoundCoverage audits obligations against immutable, authenticated
stage3t receipts. Its INPUT_STEP mode uses only original primary unit roots and
exact carried frontier proofs as donors. LOCAL_CLOSURE additionally uses all
soundly reconstructed local routes, including pure foreign intermediates that
must never enter the shared X frontier. Neither mode synthesizes a production.

For every orientation of every original clause, the audit matches opposite
signed donor literals with coherent semantic variable bindings, including
repeated variables, argument permutations and fixed values. Every enabled
conclusion must have an actually observed proof with exactly the union of the
whole donor source alternatives and the actual clause owner, at no greater than
max(donor depths)+1. A missing result, wrong source group, excessive depth or
work stop means uncovered. A smaller observed depth for the same source group
can discharge a cyclic/redundant obligation. Primary units remain real proof
roots rather than invented zero-premise applications.

All orientations must bind every head variable from other literals. For example
`@x @y r(x,y)->p(x)` is outside coverage: negative p(x) can imply negative r(x,y)
for arbitrary y. The absence of known matching constants is insufficient. This
preflight applies even to a dormant rule. Universal units and witnesses remain
outside the existing ordinary cut scope. The restriction is conservative; it is
not an assertion that every excluded rule is unsafe.

Receipts now retain an internal immutable complete local proof ledger in
addition to publicly exported X-dependent products. All cuts/self must match
the exact input object and occur exactly once, be verified and carry no collision
before any audit success is returned. The output is an audit boolean/reason,
not a publication authority, family fixed-point verdict or consistency proof.
Existing transport merge semantics and all authoring gates are unchanged.

## Why two audits, and why no admission yet

Concrete control: X has `b(x)->c(x)`; A has `a(1)` and `a(x)->b(x)`.
Input-step obligations require the observed local b(1). If the c(1) observation
is omitted, input-step alone still passes because b was not an input donor.
Local closure checks the newly proved foreign-only b and fails for missing c.
The qualification runner removes that observation from the internal audit input
to demonstrate this distinction; it does not claim native inference actually
loses c in this fixture. Public receipts cannot be caller-constructed/edited.

Signed unit closure alone does not prove consistency for arbitrary clauses.
The four ground clauses `(p or q)`, `(p or not q)`, `(not p or q)`,
`(not p or not q)` have no units, hence no enabled unit obligations, yet each
assignment of p and q falsifies one clause. Thus even a covered unchanged pool
must not grant arbitrary-clause admission. A later scoped consistency proof or
explicit rejection of uncovered clause families is required, followed by the
family executor/candidate/default-entrypoint integration. Question3 remains open.

## Verification

The new runner covers both modes, actual positive and negative native results,
omitted observations, foreign-only intermediates, wrong source groups/depths,
quantified binding/permutation/repetition/constants, unbound reverse heads,
independent sibling opposition, carried alternatives, immutable local ledgers,
exact input identity and missing/duplicate/work/depth controls. The focused
suite and Core-only artifact run are recorded in the stage3u checkpoint.

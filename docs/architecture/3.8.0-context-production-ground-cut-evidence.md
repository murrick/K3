# Stage3s: signed ordinary evidence local to X and one foreign cut

## Concrete prerequisite

For X `!@x left(x)&&right(x)->p(x);`, A `!left(1); !p(1);`,
B `!right(1); !~p(1);`, the existing family assembly still refuses because X has
no literal seed anchor. It also does not treat foreign primary facts as a global
proof pool. These are separate coverage boundaries, not evidence that raw A/B
knowledge should be merged.

ContextGroundCutEvidence reconstructs actual native applications in a fresh
private runtime containing exactly whole native-bound X and at most one foreign
snapshot. It supports a rule-only X without inventing a seed or Source. It
checks original source identities and whole captured clause correspondence,
then reuses exact native producer-instance authentication and bounded proof
closure. This is an observation/evidence API, not saturation or admission.

## Local donors and exported authority

Only actual asserted ordinary unit clauses from these two snapshots become
local proof donors, each at depth0 with its exact primary Source. Signed facts
are retained. Pure foreign-derived chains may supply local premises, but do not
become exported X knowledge. A production is exposed only when its reconstructed
proof contains an actual localX Source and depth is positive. The conclusion
must match a captured ordinary native application; the checker never invents
missing outputs. Full source alternatives and max-donor-depth+1 are retained.

No owning sibling's facts are loaded. The API does not accept a list of foreign
snapshots. Native compile/binding/capture/depth/work/event failures return an
unverified result with no proof prefix. Even a verified result with no observed
applications explicitly makes no closure claim. Universal units and witnesses
remain outside this ordinary donor surface.

## Counterexample and controls

X–B produces negative left(1) at depth1 with exactly the X rule, B right(1) and
B negative p(1) Sources. Native A rejects that actual negative literal despite
its masking positive p(1). X–A does not consume B's facts. Unrelated X with
opposite sibling p facts exports no products from either independent trial.

A purely foreign two-rule chain remains local; when its final fact feeds a rule
owned by X, the exported product has the complete four-source depth3 proof.
Direct negative heads and alternative foreign donor routes also retain their
signed separate proofs. Work/depth/event/role/witness/universal-unit boundaries,
immutable results and physical source nonmutation are checked.

## Next boundary

The existing family executor, candidate facade and default authoring have not
been redirected to this observation API. Next: common-input signed round
coverage, transport only of proved X-dependent productions, and qualification
against every independent cut. A local verified observation must not substitute
for either full round coverage or a final consistency certificate. The original
default authoring contract remains last RED56/2. Question3 and remaining language/
TRUST/new-witness coverage stay open.

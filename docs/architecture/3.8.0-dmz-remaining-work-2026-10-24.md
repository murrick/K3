## Near-term finite checkpoint

Finish the already identified conjunction and disjunction combining diagonal
and independent heads. Then consolidate the supported relational fragment into
one coverage/acceptance matrix: signed atoms, constants/argument mappings,
quantifier polarity/scope, shared/repeated/omitted parameters, compound heads,
whole Sources/alternative support, immutable rounds, actual admission/refusal,
resource stops and durable reopen. Map requirements to existing drivers and
record genuine missing interactions or counterexamples. Do not keep expanding
stages just by syntactic renaming or another permutation of covered constructs.

This is a bounded internal milestone, not a declaration of full KANGER language
support. The next functional checkpoint is ordinary-operation integration and
its compatibility gates. Unsupported required witness/function/branch forms
remain explicit work, including where they block safe integration. The full
DMZ target cannot be closed until the five acceptance packages above agree
with implementation, documented public behavior and the manual candidate.

## What the native path actually supplies

DomainFactory waiters are registered by RuleFactory.expand for SINGLE-literal
branches with variables. Ground compound rules are not such waiters. An initial
waiter-match probe observed zero interactions for X/A, so that placement was
rejected. Existing waiter assumption handling is not evidence of resolution of
the two compound rules. Hypotheses/orphans (question3) remain open.

The accepted hook is at Linker.linkDatabase after checkSystem succeeds, at an
actual native terminal branch visit. ContextProductionCapture opt-in
observeCompositionCandidates snapshots the WHOLE declared ground branch. It
compares only already visited ground branches in that same native Mind. An
opposite semantic ground literal yields a CompositionCandidate holding both
clauses, the pivot and BOTH independent lists of source Supports. No resolvent,
unit application, native rule or new fact is created. Fields are immutable and
constructors private. The pivot is syntax inside two clauses, not proved unit
knowledge. Whole conjunctive Source identity and carried alternative recipes
are preserved; depths are copied without resetting or incrementing them.

The hook uses shapeOf, which retains declared universal variables even when
native bindings happen to be present. Nonground clauses, singleton branches,
queries and nonordinary shapes are excluded. One journal may observe several
visible Minds, but branches from different Minds cannot pair. Thus this metadata
cannot silently merge independent sibling contexts.

Unique visited branch records and candidate records share the existing event
allowance. Exhaustion raises the usual capture failure/gap; raw snapshots are
not complete receipts and successful prefixes carry no authority. With opt-in
disabled the new hook does no comparison and consumes no events. No changes
were made to native matching, hypothesis preparation or inference behavior.

## Authority and next step

On the ground X/A counterexample the native traversal visits both full branches
and records one candidate. The baseline still has no composed residual. Capturing
the pair therefore distinguishes an observed obligation from an absent proved
consequence. This is not complete enumeration of clause interactions: unvisited
branches and universal unification remain outside this diagnostic scope.

Candidates are not added to residual receipts/frontiers, Horn models or general
write permissions. The old unit certificate, stage3aa reduction-only certificate
and candidate/provider/default write gates remain unchanged. Original default
contract remains last stage3r RED56/2. The next step is an authenticated proof
and coverage path for a candidate's composed consequence, including coherent
substitution and the whole union of source alternatives, before transporting
it across an immutable family barrier. Absence of candidates cannot establish
saturation.

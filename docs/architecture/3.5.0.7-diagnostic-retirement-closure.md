consolidation without weakening the permanent regression and safety gates that
protect demonstrated architectural invariants.

are no longer part of the maintained repository surface:

  — timeout/thread-dump wrapper around the existing factory concurrency
  regression;

  pass;

## Retained gates

Classes named `SafetyRunner` and the headless `KangerTestRunner` are not
investigation debris. They are executable regression gates for invariants found
and qualified during stabilization, including:

- Rule-scoped C-variable identity and selective unlink;
- Mind transaction reservation, rollback and partial-commit safety;
- factory action rollback and concurrency behavior;
- Linker checkpoint balance and Rule ordering;
- TValue resurrection and ordering;
- storage acquisition, reindex and close-failure atomicity;
- empty-index and comparable-index boundaries.

They remain intentionally available to Maven and GitHub Actions.

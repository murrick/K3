## Frame and whole-source scope

`ContextUniversalUnitClosure.close` consumes sealed native-bound snapshots of X
and at most one foreign cut, which may be an explicitly trusted group. It checks
all original inventory/Source roles, exact group members, no physical overlap
with X and a64 whole-source bound. Independent foreign contexts are not silently
merged. Result parent/target cuts preserve exact detached view identities; live
connection eligibility and provider leases remain the caller's separate duty.

All ordinary clauses of each whole row are preflighted. Initial supported shapes
are positive ground/universal units and positive definite range-restricted Horn
clauses, with at most2 variables,8 literals/arguments and8 clauses per row.
A positive conjunct does not waive an unsupported signed/multi-head/head-only
primary clause of the same row. Functions and nonordinary arguments cannot gain
authority through this API: ordinary entries are already native-bound. No
synthetic Source is assigned to an individual clause or derived theorem.

Witness bundle Sources are explicitly reported in separateWitnessSources and
produce no universal schema proof. This projection neither qualifies those
bundles nor invents X seeds to replace missing family seed ownership. A vacuous
schema projection is not a whole-family fixed point or consistency result.

## Complete bounded unit-resolution rounds

The ledger enumerates every positive unit proof, every compound Horn proof and
every opposite body pivot with matching signature, including ground pivots and
interactions absent from native callbacks. Principal flat unification is symbolic;
no database-value enumeration replaces universally quantified variables.
Nonunifiable pairs contribute no inference and never become refutations.

Each round reads one immutable input ledger. Its produced clauses/alternatives
are merged only into the next ledger; newly derived units become parents in the
following round. Closure is returned only after an unchanged exhaustive round.
The ledger retains every exact Source set and its minimum binary-resolution
proof depth. Equal clauses under alpha-renaming share an entry, but equal heads
with different source alternatives cannot discharge each other's obligations.
There is no subset-source minimization of these theorem alternatives.

Primary clauses retain their exact original Entry/clause identity, singleton
whole Source and depth0. A derived proof retains its unit parent, Horn parent
and pivot. Its Source set is the union of those parents, depth=max(parent depths)
+1. Before returning closure, a proof checker traverses the immutable DAG back
to original native-bound entries, recomputes each resolution and checks clause,
Source set and depth. Proof DAG depth here counts binary symbolic resolution,
not an assertion of the shortest possible native multi-premise inference proof.

Eliminating a universal body literal can free a head variable in a remaining
conditional rule. Such non-range-restricted derived clauses are supported only
with their checked derivation. Unsupported head-only *primary* rows remain
refused. For example forall p(u), q(7) and p(u)&&q(v)->rel(u,v) generate the
intermediate forall u,v (q(v)->rel(u,v)), then forall u rel(u,7). The universal
head variable is not replaced by a fresh constant or an existential witness.

Limits are explicit: rounds/depth<=64, clause entries<=512, alternatives per
clause<=256 and shared work budget. A new uncovered proof beyond a limit yields
unitClosed=false with no proof prefix. A longer derivation does not trigger a
false depth failure when the same exact clause/Source set already has a shorter
proof. These are operational limits, not a general termination guarantee for
KANGER or an activation of the function/mixed-prefix language.

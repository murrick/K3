# Stage3j: X seed and full-family assembly from native-bound snapshots

## No artificial productive bootstrap

ContextWitnessResultTransport.primarySeeds now builds a detached primary packet
directly from a completely native-bound X snapshot. A real initial X no longer
needs a made-up auxiliary application to get through the old product-only export
boundary. No generated cache is imported: actual X:p->warm must derive warm again
in the private native pass, with its exact original producer Source.

Every whole X witness bundle contributes its signed atoms at depth0 with its
original singleton Source. Canonical Origin is that Source plus the bundle's
existing global binder ordinal, preserving p/flag sharing. Every native-checked
ground unit clause contributes its scalar literal at depth0, with semantic fixed
slots and original source ID. Existing atom/support/rank/work bounds remain in
force. Universal units and nonunit clauses are not falsely enumerated as facts.

## Complete primary row accounting

ContextProductionFamilyAssembly validates context/revision/role/view/native-ID
membership for every bound Entry, and exactly one snapshot per Context. It builds
an immutable RowUse ledger covering every primary row of X and every foreign
snapshot, with no generated primary identities:

- X witness bundles and entirely ground-unit conjunctions become X_SEEDS.
- X nonunit/universal producer rows become X_PRODUCER declarations.
- A mixed ordinary source with ground units plus producer clauses is explicitly
  X_SEEDS_AND_PRODUCER: unit facts enter seeds, while the entire original source
  statement stays in the producer list under the same Source, not synthetic IDs.
- Every foreign primary statement, including scalar facts and whole existential
  bundles, is retained only as FOREIGN_CUT knowledge. It never enters X seeds.

The immutable Plan has a real self-X producer branch and all foreign cuts, each
with identical X declarations and its complete own primary statement list.
Scalar X seed rows are not replayed as new producer declarations. A mixed row's
unit clause may currently make its whole producer coverage incomplete; it is not
split or hidden to obtain a positive verdict.

Assembly validates the existing16-source family and declaration bounds. No
supported X seeds, no self-X producer declarations, an empty foreign snapshot,
wrong roles, repeated Contexts or seed/work limits return assembled=false with
no Artifact/prefix. Empty self/foreign-cut protocols remain future work; no dummy
rule, invented source ID or artificial empty source completes them.

## Historical artifact and guarded real-provider execution

The detached Artifact retains the complete bound X/foreign snapshots, exact
primary seed packet, full Plan, and row ledger. evaluate runs the existing family
qualification protocol and preserves COLLISION / scoped FIXED_POINT / INCOMPLETE.
It is a diagnostic of these historical snapshots, not a live authorization.

The qualification-only DUMB2 lease now has qualify(rounds,depth,work,events).
Under its DB monitor it binds all current effective views, assembles the family,
executes the diagnostic and rechecks freshness in finally. Acquisition/assembly
failures throw without a family prefix; scoped inference failures remain typed
INCOMPLETE. Closed lease authority cannot be bypassed with historical artifacts.
No user-written list of declarations or hand-supplied rule IDs is needed.

An actual-storage control reaches the scoped fixed point while preserving X's
published revision and rule count. After a private foreign scalar fact is added,
the operation returns INCOMPLETE rather than ignoring that fact. Neither native
cache results nor diagnostic products are written into physical X or a foreign pin.

## Counterexamples and coverage boundaries

The assembly runner proves that native p/flag witness sharing and exact scalar
sources survive primary seed creation; generated warm is absent initially and
later reconstructed with its real producer. out has exact X/A/B provenance at
depth2, without the unrelated scalar Source. Reversing snapshot order retains
completion, and an assembled constraint cut catches the delayed barrier2 conflict.

Foreign raw ground facts and existential declarations remain in their own cuts;
they do not masquerade as X knowledge. Negative scalar X facts remain signed in
the input and force the current positive-frontier refusal. A mixed scalar/witness
premise exposes the existing native proof-reconstruction gap as INCOMPLETE. These
are explicit unsupported cases, not claims of full ground/signed coverage.

The public low-level assembly consumes validated immutable snapshots but does not
authenticate arbitrary caller-chosen metadata. The live provider lease remains
the acquisition/freshness boundary. No prospective authoring candidate, publication
permission, general compatibility verdict or runtime admission path is introduced.
Main/Core/DB provider source are unchanged. TRUST, general signed/non-Horn/new
witness coverage and question3 remain open.

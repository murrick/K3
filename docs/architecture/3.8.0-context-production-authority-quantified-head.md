## Native scope controls

The accepted independent form is
`!@x out(x)->((@y tail(y))||(@z extra(z)));`.
Its whole captured clause has three distinct variable identities: antecedent x,
head y and head z. It is alpha-equivalent to the explicit prenex form
`!@x @y @z out(x)->tail(y)||extra(z);` and to renaming every binder. It differs
from `!@x out(x)->(@y (tail(y)||extra(y)));`, whose two heads share one variable
independent of antecedent x. The focused scope runner checks these identities,
equivalences and non-equivalence with both literal polarities.

The grammar explicitly rejects reusing `@y` in both separate head branches in
this declaration form (ParseError "Variable y duplicated"); it does not silently
identify the two binders. The focused runner catches that documented rejection
and verifies no primary assertion was imported. No lexical-shadowing support is
claimed or proposed. Only universal binders and atom polarity reversal are in
scope; existential/negated-quantifier semantics are not extended here.
